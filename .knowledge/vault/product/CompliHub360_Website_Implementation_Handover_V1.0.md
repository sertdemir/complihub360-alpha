## Non-Negotiable Brand and UX Rules

- Professional first. Human always.

- Explain simply and name technical or regulatory terms precisely.

- Do not use fear, pressure, exaggerated promises, competitor attacks, or unverified claims.

- CompliHub360 is a compliance information and provider-matching platform. It is not a law firm, accounting firm, tax adviser, customs broker, or regulatory authority.

- Users seeking professional support are not charged for using CompliHub360 to find providers.

- Provider commercial arrangements must never influence matching or ranking.

- Provider identities remain hidden during initial comparison and are revealed after the user chooses and books a consultation.

- Only information needed to process the request is shared, according to the user’s choices and the Privacy Policy.

- Use official authority sources for regulatory statements and show the authority, source link, and review/update date where applicable.

- Service availability must be qualified by country, requirement, and provider-network coverage.

## Tax & VAT

**Available in:** Germany · United Kingdom · Netherlands · France · Italy · Spain

Understand which registrations, returns, and reporting requirements may apply based on where your business is established, where goods are stored, what you sell, and who your customers are.

**You may need this when:**

- You sell goods or services in another country.

- You store inventory outside your home country.

- You are entering a new market.

**Support available:**

- Country-specific VAT registrations

- VAT returns and reporting

- OSS and IOSS registration and filings

- Threshold monitoring

- Intra-EU transactions

- Reverse-charge requirements

## EPR & Packaging

**Available in:** Germany · France · Italy · Spain · United Kingdom · Netherlands

Sell physical products online? EPR and packaging requirements may apply—even if you did not manufacture the products. Find out which registrations and other steps may be required in each market.  
  
Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) and packaging rules can apply when you sell or ship physical products into a country, including through an online marketplace or your own website. Depending on the market and product category, you may need to register, report volumes, contribute to recycling costs, meet take-back obligations, or appoint an authorized representative.

**You may need this when:**

- You sell or ship physical products through a marketplace, your own website, or another sales channel.

**Support available:**

- Packaging EPR, including LUCID

- WEEE and battery requirements

- Volume reporting

- Take-back and recycling obligations

- Eco-modulation fees

- Authorized representative services

**Tooltips:**

- EPR — Rules that place responsibility for a product’s end-of-life environmental impact on certain businesses.

- WEEE — Rules covering waste electrical and electronic equipment.

- Eco-modulation fees — Fees adjusted according to environmental characteristics such as recyclability or material use.

## Data & Privacy

**Available in:** Available globally: EU/EEA · UK · US · Canada · Latin America · Asia-Pacific · Middle East & Africa

Understand what personal data your business collects, why you are allowed to use it, and what documentation or safeguards may be required. Privacy requirements differ between countries, U.S. states, industries, and activities. They can affect how you collect, use, store, share, transfer, and protect personal data.

**You may need this when:**

- You handle information connected to customers, employees, website visitors, app users, or other individuals in one or more markets.

**Support available:**

- Privacy assessments for countries and U.S. states

- DPIAs and similar risk assessments

- Records of Processing Activities (RoPA)

- Privacy notices and data processing agreements

- International transfer safeguards

- Individual rights requests

- Breach assessment and notification

**Tooltips:**

- Personal data — Information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.

- GDPR — The European Union’s General Data Protection Regulation.

- UK GDPR — The United Kingdom’s data-protection framework based on the retained GDPR.

- DPIA — A Data Protection Impact Assessment used to evaluate higher-risk processing.

- RoPA — A Record of Processing Activities documenting how an organization handles personal data.

## Product Compliance

**Available in:** Available globally

Understand what your product may need before it can be legally and safely sold. Requirements can include testing, certification, labeling, documentation, and local representation. They depend on the product, destination, intended use, customer group, and your role in the supply chain.

**You may need this when:**

- You manufacture, import, distribute, or sell a physical product.

**Support available:**

- Product classification and applicable standards

- General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR)

- Testing, certification, and conformity assessment

- CE, UKCA, and other market markings

- Labels, warnings, and instructions

- Technical documentation and retention

- Responsible person, authorized representative, or local representative services

- Incident reporting, corrective action, and recalls

**Tooltips:**

- Placed on the market — The first time a product is made available in a particular market.

- Conformity assessment — The process used to show that a product meets applicable requirements.

- CE marking — A marking used for certain products covered by harmonized EU requirements.

- Technical documentation — Records demonstrating how a product meets applicable requirements.

- Responsible person or authorized representative — A locally established party performing defined regulatory responsibilities where required.

## Logistics & Customs

**Available in:** Available globally

Understand what may be required at the border, what a shipment may cost, and who is responsible at each stage. Cross-border goods movements can involve registrations, classification, declarations, duties and taxes, licenses, documentation, and trade reporting.

**You may need this when:**

- You import, export, store, or deliver physical goods across borders.

**Support available:**

- Customs broker and freight forwarder matching

- Importer, exporter, and EORI requirements

- HS and tariff classification

- Customs valuation and origin

- Declarations and shipping documents

- Duties and import taxes

- Import and export licenses

- Incoterms and delivery responsibilities

- Intrastat reporting

- Warehousing, fulfillment, and 3PL services

- Route-specific support

**Tooltips:**

- EORI — An Economic Operators Registration and Identification number used for customs activities in relevant markets.

- HS classification — The tariff classification assigned to goods for customs purposes.

- Customs value — The value used to calculate customs duties and certain taxes.

- Origin — The country treated as the origin of goods under applicable origin rules.

- Incoterms — Standard trade terms allocating specified delivery tasks, costs, and risks; they do not determine ownership or every form of liability.

- 3PL — A third-party logistics provider that performs services such as warehousing, fulfillment, or transportation.

## Legal Support

**Available in:** Available globally

Understand when qualified legal support may be needed and find a professional suited to the matter. CompliHub360 identifies the type of support and matches providers; it is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.

**You may need this when:**

- A contract, business decision, consumer issue, legal filing, dispute, or jurisdiction-specific matter requires qualified legal support.

**Support available:**

- Commercial contracts

- Website terms and consumer policies

- Returns, warranties, and consumer rights

- Liability matters

- Corporate and commercial law

- Employment and contractor matters

- Intellectual property and trademarks

- Disputes

- Applicable law and jurisdiction

- Local legal opinions and regulatory advice

**Tooltips:**

- Legal information — General information about legal requirements or processes; it is not advice for a specific situation.

- Legal advice — A qualified professional’s recommendation based on the facts and applicable law.

- Applicable law — The body of law governing a matter or agreement.

- Jurisdiction — The country, state, or legal authority whose courts or rules apply.

- Verified Legal Provider — An independent legal professional or firm reviewed against CompliHub360’s applicable provider criteria.

## Assessment & Risk Map

**IS THE ASSESSMENT FREE?**

> Yes. You can complete the assessment and view your Risk Map without an account or payment. A free account lets you save and update your Risk Map, see your best matches, book consultations, and receive available alerts.

**HOW PERSONALIZED IS MY RISK MAP?**

> Your Risk Map is based on the markets, business model, products or services, activities, and areas of support you select. The more complete your answers are, the more relevant the results can be. We will identify when more information is needed.

**WHAT IF I AM UNSURE ABOUT AN ANSWER?**

> You can skip questions you are unsure about, return to them later, or request professional support when confirmation is needed.

**WHAT DO THE RISK MAP STATUSES MEAN?**

> Confirmed means the requirement applies based on your answers. Likely to Apply means it probably applies, but more information or professional confirmation may be needed. More Information Needed means additional answers are required for a more accurate assessment.

**WHERE DOES THE INFORMATION COME FROM?**

> Regulatory information is based on official authority sources. Where available, we show the authority, source link, relevant date, and when the information was last reviewed.

**CAN I ASSESS SEVERAL MARKETS OR AREAS AT ONCE?**

> Yes. You can include multiple markets and areas of support in one organized Risk Map.

**CAN I CHANGE MY ANSWERS OR UPDATE MY RISK MAP?**

> Yes. Update your answers when your activities, markets, products, or business structure change.

**CAN I EXPORT MY INFORMATION?**

> You can export your Risk Map as a PDF. Depending on availability and your account features, engagement records may also be available as PDF, CSV, or through an API.

**WHAT IF MY MARKET IS NOT AVAILABLE?**

> You can request an additional market and, where available, choose to receive an alert when coverage becomes available.

## Providers & Engagements

**DOES COMPLIHUB360 PROVIDE LEGAL, TAX, ACCOUNTING, OR OTHER PROFESSIONAL ADVICE?**

> No. CompliHub360 provides compliance information and matching services. We are not a law firm, accounting firm, tax adviser, customs broker, or regulatory authority. Advice and professional services are provided independently by the selected provider.

**HOW ARE PROVIDERS REVIEWED?**

> Providers are reviewed against applicable criteria such as qualifications, expertise, market coverage, business information, experience, and service standards. Where available, we may also verify licenses, certifications, response history, reviews, and completed engagements.

**WHY ARE PROVIDER NAMES HIDDEN AT FIRST?**

> Provider names remain hidden during the initial comparison so you can focus on expertise and fit rather than brand recognition. This also gives providers of different sizes a fair opportunity to be selected.

**WHEN WILL I SEE THE PROVIDER’S NAME AND FULL PROFILE?**

> The provider’s identity and full profile are revealed after you choose the provider and book a consultation. You can then review its verified credentials, reviews, business-verification information, and contact details.

**CAN PROVIDERS PAY TO APPEAR HIGHER IN MY MATCHES?**

> No. Matching and ranking are based on relevant criteria and cannot be purchased. Advertising budgets, brand size, and commercial arrangements do not determine your best matches.

**HOW MUCH WILL THE SERVICE COST?**

> You see an expected price range before choosing a provider. After the consultation, the provider confirms the services, final price, expected timeline, and terms. Professional work begins only after you approve them.

**HOW QUICKLY WILL A PROVIDER RESPOND?**

> The provider’s expected response time is shown before you choose. If the provider does not respond within that period, CompliHub360 can suggest another match. Your information will not be shared with another provider without your approval.

**CAN I CHOOSE MORE THAN ONE PROVIDER?**

> Yes. You can select and book consultations with more than one provider. Each booking and professional engagement remains separate.

**WHAT HAPPENS AFTER I BOOK?**

> The provider’s identity and full profile are revealed, and only the information needed to process your request is shared. The provider then confirms the service, price, timeline, and terms. No professional work begins until you approve it.

**AM I REQUIRED TO CONTINUE WITH THE PROVIDER AFTER THE CONSULTATION?**

> No. Booking a consultation does not require you to begin a professional engagement.

**HOW DOES COMPLIHUB360 SUPPORT FREE ACCESS FOR USERS?**

> CompliHub360 is free for businesses seeking support. We earn revenue through our commercial relationships with participating providers. These arrangements never influence which providers appear in your matches or how they are ranked.

## Account, Data & Privacy

**DO I NEED AN ACCOUNT?**

> No account is required to ask a question, complete the assessment, or view your initial Risk Map. A free account is required to save and update results, see your best matches, book consultations, and use available workspace features.

**HOW IS MY INFORMATION USED?**

# 14. Content and Interface Standards

## Capitalization

- Use title case for primary buttons and navigation labels.

- Use sentence case for descriptions, help text, and status explanations.

- Capitalize Risk Map, Verified Provider, and CompliHub360 when used as defined platform terms.

## Language

- Use clear international English and U.S. spelling, including “authorized.”

- Preserve official technical terms such as EPR, RoPA, DPIA, EORI, HS classification, Incoterms, CE, and UKCA.

- Explain technical terms through tooltips rather than replacing them with inaccurate casual language.

- Avoid “guaranteed,” “fully compliant,” “risk-free,” “best provider,” or similar absolute claims.

- Use “may apply” and the approved status labels whenever applicability depends on additional facts.

## Accessibility

- Tooltips must be accessible by keyboard and assistive technology, not hover-only.

- Do not rely on color alone for priority or status. Include visible text labels and accessible names.

- Form errors must explain what needs to be corrected.

- Buttons and links must have distinct, descriptive labels.

- Country, region, and area selections must support keyboard navigation and clear selected states.
